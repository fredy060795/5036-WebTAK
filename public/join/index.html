What is TAK?

The Team Awareness Kit (TAK) shows your team on one shared map – live and on every device: Android (ATAK), iPhone (iTAK), Windows (WinTAK) or simply in the browser (Web EUD).

Live positionsEveryone in your team on the map, with callsign and team colour.
Markers & drawingsMark points, hostile/friendly units, routes and areas – instantly shared.
ChatMessages to everyone or to single team members.
VideoLive streams from helmet cameras, drones and phones.
Data packages & mapsMission data, overlays and offline maps from the server.
Data SyncMissions that stay up to date on all devices.
