Helmet camera on iPhone: open in "Bluefy"

The phone camera works right here. For the helmet camera (Bluetooth) the iPhone needs the free Bluefy app – normal browsers (Safari, Chrome …) cannot do Bluetooth:

  1. Get Bluefy from the App Store
  3. Open Bluefy, tap the address bar at the top, Paste and open.

Test mode on a PC: first install the test certificate and enable "full trust" under Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings (the QR code for it is in the admin under "QR / Links").
