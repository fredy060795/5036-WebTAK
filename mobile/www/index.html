WebTAK

TAK client for Android – map, team positions, chat, video and Meshtastic.

Invited by your admin?

  1. Open the invitation or your access page in the browser.
  2. Tap “Open in the WebTAK app” – server and login are taken over automatically.

Or scan the WebTAK QR code

The code from your access page (tab WebTAK) or from your admin.
